# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, accountability, and
respect for differing viewpoints. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized
conduct, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private information, or
other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful.
Report incidents privately to the repository owner. All complaints will be
reviewed promptly and fairly, with reporters' privacy respected.
